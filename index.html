FAQ

Do you log into my Discord?

By default we deliver a complete template you apply yourself. If you want us inside your server, that’s a separate hands-on option.

Does this work for my existing server?

Layouts are designed for a new server or a full reset so structure stays clean—same idea as a Discord template import, but with our channel list and notes. Fixing a live server in place is in progress and not bookable yet.

Why pay vs free templates?

Free lists rarely tie structure → engagement → monetization together. Our tiers bundle naming, roles, flows, and tier-specific patterns (VIP lanes, funnels, staff ops) with a clear deliverable and timeline—so you buy an outcome, not a bare channel list.

Can I mix channel styles?

Yes—especially on Advanced. (Professional will support more mixing when it launches.)

What about bots?

We map channels per bot; you configure the bot itself.

What format is the deliverable?

A structured server layout you implement like a Discord server template: channels, roles, naming, and written guidance (welcome flows, optional VIP/paid-access patterns by tier). We don’t run bots inside your server unless you buy hands-on help separately.

How does checkout work?

You pay through Stripe on their secure page. Our payment server emails an order summary matching your questionnaire—instant confirmation. Your full layout ships on your tier’s timeline after any quick detail check.
