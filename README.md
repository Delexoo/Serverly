## About

**Serverly** is a website that helps you design a Discord server layout without starting from scratch. Pick your goal, preview channels and roles, check out, and get a custom template with setup notes.

Good for communities, creators, and small teams who want a clean server structure fast.

---

## What you get

- Custom channel layout and categories
- Roles for staff and members
- Layout tuned to your goal (community, growth, monetization, and more)
- Written setup guidance
- Template you apply to a new or reset server

We do not log into your Discord by default. You set up the server yourself with what we send you.

---

## How it works

1. Open [serverly.store](https://serverly.store)
2. Answer a few questions in the wizard
3. Preview your layout
4. Complete checkout
5. Receive your template and instructions by email

---

## FAQ

**Do you access my Discord account?**  
No. You apply the layout yourself.

**Does this work on my current server?**  
Best for a new server or a full reset.

**What happens after I pay?**  
You get an email with your template and order details.
